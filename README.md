🏋️ FitZone - Fitness & Gym Website
📖 About the Project

FitZone is a responsive fitness and gym website that presents a gym's services and lets users interact with them. Visitors can explore why to choose the gym, browse training programs and membership plans, check their health with a BMI calculator and contact the gym. A login page leads to a personal dashboard that shows daily stats like calories burned, today's workout, water intake and weekly progress.

This project was developed as Task 4 of my internship at Skill Nexis.

✨ Features
Modern dark-themed home page with hero, about, programs and pricing sections
BMI calculator that shows your BMI and category (Underweight, Healthy, Overweight, Obese)
Login page with form validation that redirects to the dashboard
Dashboard with workout stats and progress bars for weekly goals
Sticky navbar that changes style on scroll
Scroll-reveal animations on cards
Back-to-top button and hover effects
Contact form and membership plans (Basic, Premium, Elite)
Fully responsive layout
